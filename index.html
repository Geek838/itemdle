How to play

A champion is shown. Guess the 6 items of their most commonly built item set — the community core build.

CORE ITEMThis item is part of the champion's top-6 build.
SITUATIONALHot pick, but just outside the core 6 — close!
NOT BUILTRarely built on this champion.

Find all 6 items to unlock the 🏆 Bonus Round: arrange the found items in the exact order they are most commonly purchased — from 1st item to 6th item. Green slot = right position. Unlimited attempts, but fewer is better!

  • ⭐ Daily — one champion per day, same for everyone. Share your result!
  • 🎲 Unlimited — endless random champions.
  • Builds follow the latest patch data (item list auto-synced via Data Dragon, with an offline cache fallback).
